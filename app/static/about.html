About Stock Analyser

Stock Analyser combines historical market data, technical features, company fundamentals and chronological model validation. Its forecasts are experimental statistical estimates, not guarantees.

PreviewIndex

PreviewIndex is the faster shared index. It uses up to five years of daily history, a 10-model adaptive ensemble, 30 trading-day forecasts and a six-hour refresh cycle.

FullIndex

FullIndex is the higher-capacity shared index. It uses up to ten years of daily history, 15 original models, 10 hybrids, 3 hybrids of hybrids and one primary hybrid, 30 trading-day forecasts and a separate 18-hour refresh cycle. Its shared cache is retained for 24 hours. It runs independently with smaller batches to account for the additional fitting time.

Shared adaptive learning

Both indexes maintain separate deployment-wide model-performance memories. New chronological holdout results update those memories, and later analyses use the shared performance history as a modest model-weighting prior. The memory contains aggregate model metrics, not account activity or personal information. Each stock also retains its own model-performance history.

Accuracy measurement

The 70% directional-accuracy figure is a development target, not a promised result. The site reports the measured chronological holdout accuracy for each analysis. Actual performance varies by stock, period, market regime and forecast horizon.

Data and storage

Indexed analysis output is stored once in the market index rather than duplicated in the completed job queue. Historical chart points are sampled before storage; model fitting still uses the complete downloaded history. PreviewIndex cache results expire after eight hours and FullIndex cache results after 24 hours, while compact global learning memories persist between runs.
