ABOUT THE PROJECT

About Stock Analyser

An experimental market-data indexing and stock-research application.

What it does

Stock Analyser combines a shared market-data index with an experimental ARIMA–SVR analysis model. Search and market screens let you explore indexed companies, while detailed views present historical prices, model forecasts, technical indicators, fundamentals and model diagnostics.

Indexers

RapidIndex

RapidIndex uses five years of price history and a 30-trading-day forecast horizon. It powers the shared market cache and the WatchIndex refresh process.

SlowIndex

SlowIndex uses the maximum available history and an approximately one-year forecast horizon. It is intended for a longer-horizon comparison with RapidIndex.

WatchIndex

WatchIndex checks saved watchlists across accounts. It reuses a recent RapidIndex result when one has been indexed within the six-hour cache window and refreshes stale or missing entries otherwise.

Important limitations

Forecasts are experimental statistical estimates, not calibrated guarantees or personalised recommendations. Market data may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Always check source information independently.
